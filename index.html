Fretboard Guide
Everything you need to know to get started
Chords
🎸
Chords tab — Pick a root note and quality (Major, Minor, Dom 7, etc.) to see 3 voicings: open, mid-neck, and a lush colour variant. Tap any card to see the fingering, playing tip, and strum button inline.
🎷
Jazz & Neo-Soul Chords — Shell voicings, extended harmonies (maj9, m9, 7♭9) and altered tensions. Change the root to transpose any chord. Use the search bar to filter by type.
♥
Saved Chords — Tap the ♥ button on any chord to save it. Saved chords appear in the ♥ Saved section of the Explore menu and persist between sessions.
Progressions
🎵
Progressions, Jazz Progs, NS Progs — Browse 83 progressions across 18 categories. Select a key, tap any progression to expand it and see chord diagrams. Every progression includes a song example and a ▶ Play button.
▶
Playback controls — Use the Play button to hear a progression. Toggle Loop to repeat. Adjust BPM with the slider. Switch between Strum, Travis, Boom-Chick, Waltz, and Arpeggio picking styles.
Tools
✦
Chord Story — Pick a mood and the app generates a custom progression with song examples. Tap any chord pill to swap it, use the action buttons to shift key or generate a variation, and play it back with any picking style.
⟳
Key Finder — Type any chords separated by spaces (e.g. Am G C F) and the app identifies the most likely keys. Click a result to jump straight to Progressions in that key.
⬡
Capo Finder — On the Chords tab, expand the Capo Finder. Choose a shape and a target key — it tells you exactly which fret to place your capo, and suggests alternative shapes with lower capo positions.
Audio
🔊
Audio uses the Web Audio API — no downloads required. Tap ▶ Strum on any chord detail to hear a single chord. For iOS, audio activates on the first tap. Note: on iPhone, the side silent switch will mute audio. This is an iOS browser limitation — when published as an app via Capacitor, audio will play through silent mode like any music app.
